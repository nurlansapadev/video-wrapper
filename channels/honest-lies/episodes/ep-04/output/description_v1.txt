Everyone remembers the French warship that sailed out of New York with de Gaulle's gold. No archive has a record of it.
What France actually did was quieter, and it started two years before the famous speech.

The legend of de Gaulle's gold warship has been retold for sixty years. This film goes back to the paperwork: Bretton Woods, the gold standard, the Federal Reserve vault under Manhattan, and the gold window Nixon closed in 1971. Why did France want its gold back, and why did nobody else?
