Everyone remembers the French warship that sailed out of New York with de Gaulle's gold. No archive has a record of it.
What France actually did was quieter, and it started two years before the famous speech.

Sixty years of retelling turned it into a legend. The paperwork says something else, and it runs through Bretton Woods, the gold standard, a vault several floors under Manhattan, and the day in 1971 Nixon shut the gold window. Why did France want its gold back so badly, and why did nobody else dare?
